Hello and welcome! I am a graduate student at the University of Shanghai for Science and Technology, advised by Prof. Shuai Liu. My research focuses on distributed optimization for multi-agent systems, with particular interests in privacy preservation, quantization, and communication efficiency. I have published and submitted work in privacy-preserving distributed filtering and secure distributed optimization, and am currently working on gradient tracking methods for distributed optimization.
